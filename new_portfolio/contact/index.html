    FAQ

    Questions before you write

    What should I include in a project brief for an AI developer?

    Say what you want to build and what a good result looks like, and roughly when you need it. Tell me anything I should know, such as private data, tools you already use or where it will run. Also say what you have already tried. A few lines are enough to start, and I ask for details later.

    How do we start?

    Send a short brief through the contact form or by email, as a few lines or a longer note. I read it, then reply with my view on feasibility and a proposed scope. We agree on both, and on a suitable deadline, before work begins.
