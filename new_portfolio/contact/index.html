    FAQ

    Quick answers

    Do you take on freelance projects?

    Yes. I take select freelance AI and backend projects alongside my full-time job as an AI Engineer at Claptek Pvt Ltd. I keep the number small so every client gets proper attention, and I am upfront about scope and timelines before we agree to work together.

    How much does it cost?

    I do not publish a price list, because projects vary too much. After reading your brief, I suggest either a fixed price for a clearly defined scope, or hourly billing for open-ended work. The price and the terms are agreed in writing before I start.

    How do we start?

    Send a short brief through the contact form or by email: what you want to build, roughly when you need it and anything I should know. I reply with my view on feasibility and a proposed scope. We agree on both before I write any code.

    What time zone do you work in?

    I am based in Mumbai, India, and work in Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30). I work remotely, using written updates and shared code reviews. We can agree meeting times that overlap with your working day. A past remote internship taught me how to keep distant teams in sync.
