    Process

    How a project runs

    Four steps, with short written updates along the way. No code is written until we agree on the scope.

    1. 01

      Scope

      We agree the problem and how success will be measured.

    2. 02

      Prototype

      A small working version on your data, so you see progress early.

    3. 03

      Harden

      Tests and safety checks, so it keeps working outside the demo.

    4. 04

      Hand over

      Notes and a walkthrough your team can follow to run and extend it.

    FAQ

    Before we start

    01 How do we start?

    Send a short brief through the contact form or by email: what you want to build, roughly when you need it and anything I should know. I reply with my view on feasibility and a proposed scope. We agree on both before I write any code.

    02 How much does it cost?

    I do not publish a price list, because projects vary too much. After reading your brief, I suggest either a fixed price for a clearly defined scope, or hourly billing for open-ended work. The price and the terms are agreed in writing before I start.

    03 Can you fix or extend my existing project?

    Yes. Most of my professional work has been inside software that already existed. I added an AI drafting service to a live audit product and sped up the core matching code during an internship. I read the code before changing it, keep changes tested and leave notes explaining each decision.

    04 Do you sign an NDA?

    Yes. I am happy to sign a reasonable mutual NDA (a confidentiality agreement) before we discuss private details. I do not reuse client data or code in other projects, and I only name a client or show their work in my portfolio with written approval.

    05 What time zone do you work in?

    I am based in Mumbai, India, and work in Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30). I work remotely, using written updates and shared code reviews. We can agree meeting times that overlap with your working day. A past remote internship taught me how to keep distant teams in sync.
