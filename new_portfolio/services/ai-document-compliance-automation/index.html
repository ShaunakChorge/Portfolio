    Document data extraction with AI

    Audit and compliance teams do not need Artificial Intelligence (AI) that merely sounds right. They need answers they can check. I build tools where every result points to its evidence, and I test them on your own documents.

    I build document data extraction with AI: tools that read PDFs, forms and reports and turn them into clean, checkable data. Every extracted fact links back to the exact quote in your document. When AI writes a draft, code checks it against the original notes. I measure accuracy on your own files.

    Who it is for

    Who this is for

    • Compliance and risk teams

      You read long circulars, permits and regulations to list the duties in them. You need a list where every duty links to the exact passage, so a reviewer can verify each one quickly.

    • Internal audit teams

      Findings start as short checklist notes. You want clear first drafts, in bulk when needed, with checks in code that block any figure, date or owner the auditor did not write.

    • Reporting and data teams

      Answers sit in a very large database that few people can query. You want plain questions turned into queries, plus an accuracy test that runs them, so you know how far to trust the results.

    • Product teams adding AI to an app

      You run a live product and want drafting or extraction added without rebuilding it. A separate service with a clear data agreement can be updated on its own.

    Problems I solve

    Problems I solve

    • ProblemAI page numbers and citations cannot be trusted

      How I approach itThe AI copies out the sentence it relied on; code finds it in the PDF and reads the page from the document. The AI's own page numbers were wrong 56% of the time; now 97.2% of 1,266 production records link to an exact passage.

    • ProblemAI drafts include figures, dates or names nobody wrote

      How I approach itCode compares each draft with the source note and rejects it if it adds a figure, date or owner. On the audit drafting service, 0 invented facts were found across 27 checked draft versions.

    • ProblemNobody knows how accurate the output really is

      How I approach itI build the scoring step first and run it on a sample of your documents. On the database project, simple scoring suggested about 93% quality, but running the queries showed a far lower true figure.

    • ProblemLong documents are too big to read in one go

      How I approach itI split the document at page boundaries, extract from each piece and rejoin duties that cross a break. I also measure where processing time goes: the changes that helped cut a six-document benchmark from 80.4 to 60.0 minutes.

    • ProblemA new AI tool must fit the product you already run

      How I approach itI build it as a separate FastAPI service with a clear data contract: an agreed shape for what it sends and receives. The audit service was connected to an existing Spring Boot and React product, and the client adopted it the day after handover.

    Deliverables and fit

    What you receive

    Deliverables

    • A service that reads your documents and returns clean, structured data
    • Evidence links: each fact tied to the exact quote it came from
    • Automatic checks that reject AI-written text containing invented details
    • Bulk processing for spreadsheets or whole folders of documents
    • A scoring tool that measures accuracy on a sample of your own documents
    • A review screen or export (web page or spreadsheet), if you need one

    What I need from you

    • Examples of the documents you handle, awkward ones included, and a list of the facts you want pulled from each.
    • A small set of correct answers: documents your team has already read and marked up by hand, which I use to score accuracy.
    • Where documents may be processed (your servers, your cloud account or an online AI service) and any privacy rules that apply.
    • How reviewers work today, such as spreadsheets or an existing product, so the output fits their routine and approval steps.

    The process

    How a document project runs

    1. 01

      Scope

      We pick the facts that matter and collect a small set of correct answers from your documents.

    2. 02

      Baseline

      A first working version plus a scoring tool, so every later change is judged on numbers.

    3. 03

      Improve

      Fixes aimed at the mistakes the scoring tool finds, not at guesses.

    4. 04

      Hand over

      The service, tests, scoring scripts and notes your team can rerun.

    Technology

    Technology, and where it fits

    FastAPI and Pydantic
    Serve the extraction service as an API and check every result against an agreed shape, such as allowed categories.
    PyMuPDF
    Opens PDFs and finds where a quoted passage sits, so the page number and highlight come from the document, not the AI.
    Local AI models (Ollama, vLLM)
    Read the text and return structured fields, hosted on your own servers so documents and prompts stay in-house.
    TF-IDF similarity search
    A word-weighting method that finds similar past examples, such as earlier findings, to show the AI how your team writes.
    React and pdf.js
    Show the PDF beside the extracted list with the evidence highlighted, so a reviewer can check each item against its source.
    SQL databases (Oracle)
    Used by the accuracy test: each generated query is run for real, and its rows are checked against a known-correct query.
    pytest and scoring scripts
    Run automatic tests, and score the output against correct answers from your documents after every change.
    Docker
    Packages the service with its settings, so your team can run it on its own machines the same way I do.

    Decide together

    Trade-offs we decide together

    • Find every item or only sure ones

      Tuning to catch every item raises recall (the share of items found) but adds wrong extras. Tuning for certainty raises precision (the share of found items that are correct) but misses items. I ask which mistake costs you more, then report both on your documents.

    • Bigger AI model or faster answers

      A larger model may read more carefully but is slower and needs a stronger machine. I compare candidate models on your documents against a time limit you set, as I did with four models on the audit drafting service.

    • Fully automatic or reviewed by a person

      For compliance and audit work I keep a person in charge. Each item links to its source, and a reviewer can accept, edit or reject it. Full automation only suits low-risk fields where accuracy has been measured.

    FAQ

    Questions about document extraction

    What is document AI?

    Document AI is software that reads documents such as PDFs, forms and reports. It turns what they say into structured data you can search, check and use. It can pull out names, dates, amounts and obligations. I build it so every extracted fact links back to the exact quote in the source document.

    How much does document AI cost?

    It depends on the number and variety of your documents, the accuracy you need and where the tool must run. I do not publish a price list. After a short brief and a test on a sample of your files, I suggest a fixed price or hourly billing. We agree it in writing before I start.

    How accurate is AI data extraction from PDFs?

    Accuracy is measured on your own files and reported plainly. I build a scoring tool that compares the output with a small set of correct answers from your documents, then fix the mistakes it finds. On a regulatory project, 97.2% of 1,266 production records link to an exact passage; that measures traceability, not completeness.

    Can my documents stay on our own servers?

    Yes, and that is a common requirement. Several systems I work on use AI models hosted on the client's own servers, so documents, prompts and usage records stay on the machine. During planning we check what hardware is available, because larger models need stronger machines.

    How do you stop AI from adding invented details?

    With checks in code, not just instructions in a prompt. In the audit drafting service I built, any figure, date or owner that is not in the source note causes the draft to be rejected automatically. In 27 checked draft versions, a small sample, I found no invented facts. A reviewer still accepts, edits or rejects every draft.

    Can this connect to our existing product?

    Yes. I connected an AI drafting service to a live product built with Spring Boot and React. It runs as a separate FastAPI service. There is a clear agreement about the data it sends and receives. The main product and the new service can be updated separately.

    Will the AI find every item in a long document?

    No. In a controlled comparison on documents held back from tuning, recall (how many of the real obligations were found) rose from 32% to 69% on Basel III rules and from 69% to 82% on KYC (know-your-customer) rules. Some are still missed, so every result links to its source and a person reviews the list.

    Can it read scanned pages and invoices?

    PDFs that already contain typed text are the easiest. Scanned pages and photos first need text recognition (OCR), and results depend on scan quality, so I test a sample of your scans before agreeing a plan. My case studies here cover regulatory PDFs, audit notes and a database, not invoices.
