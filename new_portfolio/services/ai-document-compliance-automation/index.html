    FAQ

    Questions about document extraction

    What is document AI?

    Document AI is software that reads documents such as PDFs, forms and reports. It turns what they say into structured data you can search, check and use. It can pull out names, dates, amounts and obligations. I build it so every extracted fact links back to the exact quote in the source document.

    How much does document AI cost?

    It depends on the number and variety of your documents, the accuracy you need and where the tool must run. I do not publish a price list. After a short brief and a test on a sample of your files, I suggest a fixed price or hourly billing. We agree it in writing before I start.

    How accurate is AI data extraction from PDFs?

    I do not promise a number before I see your files. I build a scoring tool that compares the output with a small set of correct answers from your documents, then fix the mistakes it finds. On one database project, running the generated queries showed that simple scoring had overstated quality.

    Can my documents stay on our own servers?

    Yes, and that is a common requirement. Several systems I work on use AI models hosted on the client's own servers, so documents, prompts and usage records stay on the machine. During planning we check what hardware is available, because larger models need stronger machines.

    How do you stop AI from adding invented details?

    With checks in code, not just instructions in a prompt. In a drafting service I built, any figure, date or owner that is not in the source note causes the draft to be rejected automatically. Reviewers can still accept, edit or reject every draft that remains.

    Can this connect to our existing product?

    Yes. I connected an AI drafting service to a live product built with Spring Boot and React. It runs as a separate FastAPI service. There is a clear agreement about the data it sends and receives. The main product and the new service can be updated separately.
