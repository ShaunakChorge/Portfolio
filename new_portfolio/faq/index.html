    AI developer cost in India: freelance FAQ

    AI developer cost in India depends on the scope. Shaunak Chorge agrees either a fixed price or hourly billing for each project, after a short brief and before any work begins. He takes select projects alongside his full-time job as an AI Engineer at Claptek Pvt Ltd, and answers the common questions below.

    Hire me See services

    Working together

    Can you fix an existing AI project?

    Yes. Most of my professional work has been inside software that already existed. I added an AI drafting service to a live audit product and sped up the core matching code during an internship. I read the code before changing it, keep changes tested and leave notes explaining each decision.

    Python backend and API work

    Which tools do you use?

    I work mostly in Python. For web services I use FastAPI and PostgreSQL, packaged with Docker (so it runs the same on any server). For AI I use services such as Groq and OpenAI, plus ChromaDB for document chatbots. For vision and speech I use OpenCV, PyTorch and TensorFlow. The full list is on the About page.

    The full list of tools on the About page

    Can the AI run on our own servers instead of an online service?

    Yes. At work I build on AI language models (LLMs) hosted on the client's own servers. This suits systems where no data, prompt or usage record may leave the building. My personal projects use online services such as Groq. We choose based on your privacy needs, cost and answer quality.

    AI chatbot and document assistant work

    Can a freelancer sign an NDA?

    Yes. I am happy to sign a reasonable mutual NDA (a confidentiality agreement) before we discuss private details. I do not reuse client data or code in other projects. I only name a client or show their work in my portfolio with written approval.

    Get in touch

    Who owns the code written by a freelancer?

    The ownership and licence terms are written into our agreement before work begins. The usual arrangement is that you own what you pay for. My freelance work is kept separate from my full-time job and never uses my employer's code or data.

    Get in touch

    Getting started

    What does a project look like from start to finish?

    First we agree on the problem and how success will be measured. Then I build a small working prototype on your data. Next I add tests and safety checks. Finally I hand over notes and a walkthrough. You see working software early and get short written updates along the way.

    How an engagement runs

    What time zone does a Mumbai freelance AI developer work in?

    I am based in Mumbai, India, and work in Indian Standard Time (IST, UTC+5:30). I share written updates and code reviews, so you can always see where the work stands. We can agree meeting times that overlap with your working day. Earlier internships taught me how to keep distant teams in sync.

    Contact and local time

    Cost and timelines

    How much does it cost to hire an AI developer in India?

    I do not publish a price list, because projects vary too much. After reading your brief, I suggest either a fixed price for a clearly defined scope, or hourly billing for open-ended work. The price and the terms are agreed in writing before I start.

    Ask for a proposal

    How long does a freelance AI project take?

    It depends on the size of the project. After reading your brief, I give a written estimate for each step. A small prototype is much quicker than a full product. I give you a clear range, and we agree dates in writing before work begins.

    Send a brief for an estimate

    After delivery

    What happens after you hand the project over?

    Handover includes a README (a how-to guide), setup instructions, tests and a walkthrough, so your team can run and extend the work. A bug-fix period and ongoing maintenance can be agreed as part of the scope, and I will write down exactly what is covered.
