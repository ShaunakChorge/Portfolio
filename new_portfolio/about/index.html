    Background

    Who I am and what I do

    I am a Bachelor of Technology graduate with more than a year of industry experience, and I am currently an AI Engineer at Claptek Pvt Ltd. I work mostly in Python on Artificial Intelligence (AI) and backend systems. By day I build AI-powered tools and features for audit and compliance software in the banking, financial services and insurance sector. My work includes drafting reports, reading documents and regulations with the sources shown, and answering questions from data in plain language. These tools include a document reader that points to its evidence and an assistant that turns plain questions into database queries on a bank's own servers.

    Before that, my internships showed me how real systems behave. At Osource Global I built backend APIs and document tools that use AI models running on company machines. At Shivohini Tech-AI I built AI chatbots for clients. Outside work I build whole projects myself. They include a chatbot that cites its sources or admits it does not know, and a backend that shows where each request slows down. I also built a scam-fighting AI with Prasanna Patil, plus speech and vision models inside small apps.

    Need a chatbot, a document reader, a computer vision prototype or a Python backend built carefully? Reach me through the contact page, the fastest way to start.
